Works as plain Solr search without JavaScript; skills-search.js adds results while typing, "/" and type-anywhere, Esc, arrow keys, and loads filters in place. Structure: form, live status line (outside the part that is replaced), then [data-skillflow-search-results]: topics, filters and sorting, active filters, the selected topic as a pack, the list or the empty state, paging, and the packs of all topics on the unfiltered list.
